## Beschrijving (lange versie voor PH pagina)

```
# Goaltje — World Cup 2026 Prediction Pools

The World Cup 2026 is coming — the biggest in history with 48 countries and 104 matches across the USA, Mexico, and Canada.

Goaltje makes it easy to create prediction pools with friends, family, or your entire company. No football knowledge needed — just pick a score and see who wins.

## 🔮 How it works
1. Create a free account
2. Start or join a pool (via QR code or invite link)
3. Predict scores for all 104 matches
4. Watch the live leaderboard update in real-time

## 📊 Smart Scoring
- Exact score: 6 points 🎯
- Correct goal difference: 4 points
- Correct result (W/D/L): 3 points
- Customizable per pool

## ⚡ Key Features

**For Everyone**
- Predict all 104 World Cup 2026 matches
- Live scores updated automatically
- Real-time leaderboard with streaks and badges
- Knockout bracket with Route to Final
- Pool chat with emoji reactions
- Confetti celebration for exact scores
- Available in 4 languages (NL/EN/ES/PT)
- Installable as a phone app (PWA)

**For Companies**
- White-label pools with your branding
- Custom logo, colors, and company name
- Restrict access to your email domain
- Perfect for team building during the World Cup

**Privacy & Security**
- Row Level Security on all data
- GDPR-compliant with cookie consent
- No tracking, no ads, no data selling

## 💰 Pricing
Free. Completely free. No premium tier, no paywall, no ads. All features are available to everyone.

## 🛠️ Built with
React, TypeScript, Vite, Supabase, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion, Vercel
```
